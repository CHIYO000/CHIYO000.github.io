## Research Interests

My research interests center on how people communicate with and make sense of emerging technologies across cultural contexts.

I am particularly interested in:

- Human-centered AI and user experience
- AI product evaluation and user research
- Cross-cultural communication between China and Japan
- Media, platform, and social discourse
- Localization and cultural adaptation of AI products

## Research Approach

My background in advertising, communication, and media research informs how I approach AI products: combining qualitative user understanding with structured analysis to identify user needs, communication patterns, and opportunities for product improvement.

I am especially interested in research that can connect **user insight, cultural context, and product decisions**.

> This page is intentionally focused on research interests and approach rather than presenting individual academic papers or thesis materials.
