## Overview

I work on the localization of AI products for the Japanese market, connecting product requirements, AI model behavior, Japanese language, and user expectations.

## My Approach

Localization is not limited to translating product copy. For AI products, the model itself is part of the user experience, so localization also requires adapting how the AI communicates and behaves.

### Japanese AI Interaction

- Design and refine Japanese-language Prompts.
- Adjust politeness, tone, conversational style, and response behavior for Japanese users.
- Test how different model and Prompt configurations affect the quality of interaction.

### Model Testing & Feedback

- Test different AI models and compare their performance in Japanese contexts.
- Identify issues that affect naturalness, consistency, and user experience.
- Provide structured feedback on required model capabilities and improvement areas.

### User Research

- Use interviews and product testing to understand Japanese users' reactions and expectations.
- Translate qualitative observations into product and model requirements.
- Support iterative optimization based on user feedback.

### Cross-Cultural Collaboration

- Support communication between China-based product/R&D teams and Japan-side teams.
- Help connect local market observations with product and AI development decisions.

## What I Explore

**How can an AI product feel native rather than simply translated?**

This work allows me to explore localization as a product-development process: understanding users, evaluating AI behavior, designing interaction rules, and feeding market-specific insights back into the product and model.
