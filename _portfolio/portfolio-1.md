## Overview

Fuzozo is an AI companion product designed to combine a physical companion experience with conversational AI. My work focuses on improving the quality of its Japanese user experience from both the product and model perspectives.

## My Role

I work across AI model evaluation, user research, Prompt design, and Japanese-market localization.

### AI Model Testing & Evaluation

- Test and compare different AI models used behind the product.
- Evaluate Japanese dialogue quality, naturalness, consistency, and alignment with the product's intended personality.
- Identify model-side issues and translate testing results into actionable feedback for model and product iteration.

### Prompt Design & Tuning

- Design and iteratively refine Japanese dialogue Prompts.
- Tune personality, response style, context handling, and conversational behavior.
- Evaluate how Prompt changes affect the consistency and naturalness of model outputs.

### User Research & Product Feedback

- Conduct interviews and product tests to understand user reactions and expectations.
- Organize testing observations into structured feedback for product iteration.
- Support UI/UX and interaction-flow optimization based on user needs.

### Japan Market Localization

- Adapt product content and conversational behavior for Japanese users.
- Consider Japanese language conventions, cultural context, and user expectations during localization.
- Support communication and collaboration with the Japan-side team.

## What I Explore

This project sits at the intersection of **AI Product, User Research, Model Evaluation, Prompt Design, and CN-JP Localization**.

I am particularly interested in how model capabilities and product-level design can work together to create AI interactions that feel natural and culturally appropriate.
