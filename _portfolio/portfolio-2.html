The project gave me experience in evaluating AI-generated interview interactions and considering how model outputs can be translated into a practical assessment experience for Japanese users. ## My Role - Participate in internal product testing and evaluation. - Test AI interview and Japanese-language interaction quality from a user perspective. - Examine whether model outputs are natural, appropriate, and consistent with the intended evaluation context. - Collect and organize user-testing feedback to support product improvement. - Contribute to Japanese-language and Japan-market adaptation. ## Product Research Focus My work emphasizes the connection between **AI model capability and user-facing product quality**.
